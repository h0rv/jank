Limits

  • Ahead of time only. No REPL, eval or general browser API layer, and no threads.
  • Startup fails without the explicit static roots. General GC safety is not proven.
  • Native exceptions that hold GC-managed objects are not rooted.
  • This is not general Wasm support for jank and is not part of upstream jank.
