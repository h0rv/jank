jank-wasm

A research fork of jank that compiles Clojure ahead of time to WebAssembly.

The module was compiled ahead of time from jank with Emscripten; there is no REPL or eval. It needs memory64 and Wasm exceptions: Chrome 156 runs it, Safari probably will not. To build it yourself, see the build notes.

TargetResult

wasm32-wasip1, WasmtimeRuns the spike, 20 of 20 runs with GC on. nREPL is left out.
ExceptionsThe runtime roots thrown jank objects and errors, including rethrows and copied exception_ptrs.
SpeedSlower than ClojureScript on most benchmark kernels; native fib27 is faster.

Limits

  • Ahead of time only. No REPL, eval or general browser API layer, and no threads.
  • Startup fails without the explicit static roots. General GC safety is not proven.
  • Native exceptions that hold GC-managed objects are not rooted.
  • This is not general Wasm support for jank and is not part of upstream jank.

Details and evidence are in the experiment notes.
